Connector accessories vs BOM

For each connector, the app works out what it needs (backshell size from the shell size, catalog contacts, sealing plugs for unused cavities, bands and their tool) from sourced reference data, and compares that with the BOM currently in this project, whether extracted, imported or typed. MISMATCH = the BOM contradicts the reference; MISSING = not found in the BOM; VERIFY = cannot be decided yet (e.g. populated cavities not documented). Nothing here approves a part.
